# Level Brief — "First Steps", tutorial room

**Difficulty baseline.** This is the easy case: if the swarm cannot pass this
without a repair round, the skills need work.

## Required beats
1. Flat ground across the bottom of the screen.
2. Two platforms, both trivially reachable.
3. One collectible on the second platform.
4. Nothing else. No hazards, no detours, no decisions.

## Feel
Default tuning is fine. Do not get clever.

## Constraints
- Single screen: 1152 x 648. Player is 32 x 48.
